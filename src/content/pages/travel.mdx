## Overland travel

Your party can travel **one hex** per **Watch**. **Difficult terrain** (mountains, swamp, etc.) require **two Watches** per hex traveled.

### Foraging

In the wild, your character can spend a Watch **foraging**.

If they do, they will find **d2 (d4 / 2) uses** of **rations**.

# Weather

Roll 2d6 each morning. If spring or fall, subtract 1 from sum. If winter, subtract 2.

| 2d6 modified | Weather              |
| ------------ | -------------------- |
| 2 or less    | Storm                |
| 3-5          | Rain, sleet, or snow |
| 6-7          | Clouds               |
| 8-12         | Clear                |

## Windy 

If the two dice match (doubles), the day is windy. 

Both dice are an even number? Shift wind direction clockwise one step. Odd? Counterclockwise one step. 

## Bad Weather

In a storm or when both windy and raining/sleeting/snowing, all terrain becomes difficult. Any Watch spent traveling forces a Strength save to avoid taking an Exhaustion. Max one save per traveling day.
