## 1. Attributes

Your character has three attribute scores. These measure their basic strengths and weaknesses.

- **STR:** physical strength and resilience.
- **DEX:** speed and agility.
- **WIL:** strength of will and charisma.

For each of these attributes, in order, roll **3d6**.

Keep the **two highest dice** results for a value between 2—12.

You may then swap any two attributes.

## 2. HP, Coins and Background

**Roll d6** for your **Hit Protection** (HP). This is the damage your character can shrug off before taking serious harm.

**Roll 2d6** for your starting **coins**. These are the basic currency of the settled places of the world.

## 3. Starting equipment

Your character starts with:

- Torches
- Rations
- A light weapon of your choice
- Supply
- Bedroll
