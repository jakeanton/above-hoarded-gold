## Inventory slots

Your character carries items in their inventory slots. Most items take up one inventory slot. Some larger items, such as two-handed weapons and armour take up two slots.

### Hand slots

Your character has two hand slots. Items in these slots are carried.

### Body slots

Your character has two body slots. Worn items can be swapped with on-hand slots as a free action.

### Pack slots

Your character has eight pack slots. Items in these slots take time to retrieve when under pressure. While in combat, your character must use an action (instead of attacking) to find an item in their pack.

## Conditions

Conditions are **negative effects** suffered by your character. Each Condition must be placed in an **inventory slot**. PCs may have multiple copies of the same condition.

Some Conditions have additional effects, which apply as long as the Condition remains in your inventory.

Conditions can only be **removed** by meeting their **clear** requirement—usually a short, long or full rest.

## Usage

Most items have three **usage dots**. When all three dots are marked on an item it is depleted or destroyed. Usage dots can be cleared from weapons/armour for 10% of the original cost per dot cleared.

- **Weapons/armour/ammunition:** after a fight, roll d6 for each item that was used during the fight. On 4-6, mark usage.
- **Torches/lanterns:** mark usage every 6 Turns.
- **Rations:** mark usage after a meal.
- **Other gear:** if used in a way that could break or deplete it, the GM may ask you to mark usage.

## Encumbrance

If your character is carrying more items or Conditions than available inventory slots, they are **encumbered**.

While encumbered, your character cannot **run**, and makes all saves with **Disadvantage**.

## Weapons

### Melee weapons

#### Improvised

**d6 damage**  
Wielded in: Main paw/both paws  
Special rule: Always mark usage after a fight.

#### Light

**d6 damage**  
Wielded in: Main hand or off hand  
Special rule: If attacking with two weapons, roll both dice and use the best result.  
Example: Dagger, hatchet, shortsword

Cost: 10p

#### Medium

**d6/d8 damage**  
Wielded in: Main hand or both hands
Special rule: Does d6 damage in one paw, d8 wielded in both.  
Example: Sword, axe

Cost: 20p

#### Heavy

**d10 damage**  
Wielded in: Both hands  
Example: Greatsword, spear, warhammer

Cost: 40p

### Ranged weapons

#### Light ranged

**d6 damage**  
Wielded in: Main hand  
Example: Sling, light crossbow

Cost: 10p

#### Heavy ranged

**d8 damage**  
Wielded in: Both hands  
Example: Bow, heavy crossbow

Cost: 40p

### Ammunition

#### Stones, pouch

Carried in: Body slot

Cost: 1p

#### Arrows, quiver

Carried in: Body slot

Cost: 5p

## Armour

Reduces damage dealt to your character by enemy attacks.

### Light armour

**Prevents 1 damage**  
Worn in: Off paw and one body slot

Cost: 150p

### Heavy armour

**Prevents 1 damage**  
Worn in: Two body slots

Cost: 500p

## Other items

### Torches and lanterns

**Provide light**

Light sources mark usage every 6 Turns.

Lanterns must be refilled by oil.

### Rations

**Food for the road**

Eating a ration and spending a Watch resting will heal all HP.

If your character doesn’t eat for a day, they gain a Hungry Condition.

### Coin Purse

**Holds 250 coins**

Carried coins require one inventory slot per 250, barring the first 250, which are carried in your PC's pockets. Settlements and travelers deal in a mix of purchase, barter, and IOUs.
